# Legal & Regulatory Compliance Standards (US & California)

Behavioral checks and architectural constraints to protect against statutory liabilities and fines under United States and California laws.

---

## 1. Age Verification & Child Privacy (COPPA)
*Statutory authority: 15 U.S.C. §§ 6501–6506 (FTC penalties up to $53,000+ per violation)*

- **Neutral Age Gate / DOB Screen**: Any product with public user registration, user profiles, or personal data collection (email, real name, avatar) must include a neutral age gate or birth-year verification before collecting PII.
- **Under 13 Protection**: If a user indicates they are under 13, immediately block collection of personal information and do not persist identifiers without verifiable parental consent.
- **Children's Online Safety**: Never collect persistent identifiers (device IDs, advertising IDs, IP addresses for behavioral profiling) from children without compliant flows.

---

## 2. Session Replay & Wiretapping Laws (California CIPA)
*Statutory authority: Cal. Penal Code § 631 (California Invasion of Privacy Act - $5,000 statutory damages per session)*

- **No Unauthorized Keystroke / Interaction Interception**: Do not introduce third-party session replay scripts (e.g., FullStory, Hotjar, Microsoft Clarity, LogRocket) without explicit, prior, affirmative opt-in consent from the user.
- **Input Masking**: If analytics or diagnostic logging is used, all input fields, passwords, search bars, and PII must be masked (`[data-private]`, `.mask()`) at the capture level so raw customer keystrokes are never transmitted or stored.

---

## 3. Email Communications (CAN-SPAM Act)
*Statutory authority: 15 U.S.C. § 7701 (FTC penalties up to $53,000+ per email)*

All outbound emails generated by the application (transactional confirmations, reminders, announcements, notifications via Resend, SendGrid, Postmark, AWS SES, etc.) must include:
1. **Valid Physical Postal Address**: A physical postal address or registered PO Box of the organization in the footer of every email template.
2. **Clear Identification**: Accurate header and "From" information clearly stating who is sending the message.
3. **One-Click Unsubscribe / Opt-Out**: A visible, operational unsubscribe link or preference center for any non-purely-transactional communications, honored within 10 business days.

---

## 4. Subscriptions & Recurring Charges (California Auto-Renewal Law - ARL)
*Statutory authority: Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code §§ 17600–17606*

When implementing billing, checkout flows, or subscription models (e.g., Stripe, LemonSqueezy):
- **Clear & Conspicuous Notice**: The recurring nature, exact billing cycle, recurring amount, and automatic renewal terms must appear directly adjacent to the payment submission button (before customer clicks to purchase).
- **Easy Cancellation**: Provide an immediate, simple, online mechanism to cancel (e.g., 1-click customer portal or cancellation button in settings). Under California law, a user who subscribes online must be allowed to cancel online without friction.
- **Acknowledgment & Post-Purchase Notice**: Immediately send a confirmation email with full cancellation terms and instructions.

---

## 5. Third-Party CDNs & IP Address Privacy (Google Fonts / Munich Precedent)
*Legal context: Munich Regional Court (Az. 3 O 17493/20), CCPA / CPRA*

- **Self-Host Fonts & Assets**: Do not use remote CDN font imports (`https://fonts.googleapis.com`, `https://fonts.gstatic.com`) in HTML/CSS templates or web views. Remote CDN requests transmit user IP addresses to third-party servers without user consent.
- **Bundling**: Package fonts and static resources locally (`@font-face` pointing to local assets or bundled in the application assets).

---

## 6. User-Generated Content & Copyright (DMCA Safe Harbor)
*Statutory authority: 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(2)*

- If an application permits users to upload, post, or share content (images, audio, text, files) that is visible to other users or the public:
  1. A designated DMCA agent must be registered with the U.S. Copyright Office ($6 directory fee).
  2. Public Terms of Service and Copyright notices must publish the designated agent's contact details.
  3. A defined notice-and-takedown procedure must exist before launch.
